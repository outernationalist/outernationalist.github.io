Stack Drawings

October 2007
Randomly generated drawings

Stack Drawings are a series of drawings that explore stack order as a way to achieve spatial depth. Fragments of drawings that evoke interiors, shadows, and structures are randomly composed using PHP. Hovering your mouse hides and reveals parts of the stack to manipulate the space, one element at a time. Structures are revealed through slow, successive, movement through the forms.
