Global Psychedelic Tapestries

August - September 2006
Dry point, hard-ground etching, ink

global psychedelic tapestry
-noun

a richly and complexly designed universal cloth woven by a profound sense of intensified sensory perception, sometimes accompanied by severe perceptual distortion and hallucinations pertaining to the whole world

Source: Dictionary.com Unabridged (v 1.1)

These works explore recursion as a mostly digital, sometimes analog, collage process. This interplay between organic and digital is evident not only in the process and materials, but also in the content.

Recursion in plain English, from Wikipedia:
“Recursion is the process a procedure goes through when one of the steps of the procedure involves rerunning the procedure. A procedure that goes through recursion is said to be recursive.”
